1. PROBLEM STATEMENT & THE "WHY"
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Standard weather applications and websites provide raw numbers (such as 32°C, 82% humidity,
12 km/h wind) which are confusing and non-actionable for ordinary citizens, farmers, pilots,
and disaster response authorities. Furthermore:
1. Rural farmers cannot interpret meteorological parameters to make critical spraying,
   irrigation, or sowing decisions.
2. In disaster scenarios (cloudbursts, lightning strikes, localized flash floods), real-time
   plain-language early warnings in regional Indian languages are almost non-existent.
3. Offline/feature-phone users with 2G/3G connectivity are completely excluded from modern
   visual weather platforms.
4. Conventional chatbots overwrite the user's dashboard location or hallucinate outdated
   information without real satellite/synoptic ground truth.

WeatherGPT solves this by transforming multi-source spaceborne and terrestrial atmospheric
telemetry into actionable, vernacular, role-tailored decision intelligence.

2. COMPLETE FEATURE BREAKDOWN & TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[FEATURE 1: 3D WebGL Planetary Earth Globe]
- Implementation: Three.js WebGL canvas rendering a 3D Earth sphere with custom atmospheric
  scattering shaders, realistic lighting vectors, and rotating cloud layers.
- Real-Time Precipitation Engine: Dynamic particle system simulating rain, cloud cover, and
  clear sky lighting synced to the user's active geographic coordinates.
- UI Ergonomics: Dockable and minimizable summary card ensuring zero obstruction of the 3D
  planet.

[FEATURE 2: Dual-Tier LLM Intelligence Pipeline with Multi-Turn Memory]
- Tier 1 (Primary): Groq Cloud executing LLaMA 3.3 70B Versatile, delivering complete
  meteorological reasoning in <500ms.
- Tier 2 (Fallback): Google Gemini 1.5/2.5 Flash providing automatic failover if Groq hits
  rate-limits or network timeout.
- Decoupled Architecture: Asking about "Weather in Mumbai" in the chat drawer answers the
  question in the conversation history without disturbing or overriding the main dashboard's
  active location (e.g. Ghaziabad).
- Role Personas: Dynamic system prompting tailored for Farmers (Gramin Krishi Mausam Seva),
  Pilots (METAR/TAF/VFR/Turbulence), Disaster Teams (NDMA protocols), and Public Health.

[FEATURE 3: Full-Stack Vernacular Multi-Lingual Architecture (7 Indian Languages)]
- Supported Languages: English, हिन्दी (Hindi), मराठी (Marathi), தமிழ் (Tamil),
  తెలుగు (Telugu), বাংলা (Bengali), and ગુજરાતી (Gujarati).
- Total System Localization: When a user selects a language:
  a. UI headers, buttons, cards, and tooltips switch to native regional script.
  b. Active weather reports and metrics re-fetch in that regional language.
  c. LLM chat answers are generated directly in the vernacular script.
  d. Text-To-Speech (TTS) voice synthesizer reads bulletins in native regional dialect.

[FEATURE 4: DAMINI / IITM Real-Time Lightning & Convective Instability Risk Analyzer]
- Genuine Meteorological Convective Analysis: Analyzes thermal energy, dew-point depression,
  rain probability, and storm codes to compute real convective instability.
- Plain-Language Classification:
  * 🟢 SAFE: Stable atmosphere. Zero convective storm cells within 30km radius.
  * 🟡 CAUTION: Convective cloud buildup in progress. Watch for sudden thunder/rain.
  * 🔴 DANGER: Active lightning discharge. Immediate indoor shelter advisory.
- Ground Sensor Metrics: Displays nearest strike distance (km), 30-minute discharge counts,
  and convective cell movement vectors.
- NDMA 30-30 Safety Directive: Actionable safety guidelines for rural field workers.

[FEATURE 5: ISRO INSAT-3DR Geostationary Satellite & Synoptic Radar Visualizer]
- Spaceborne Satellite Imagery: Embedded geostationary sector feeds (74°E orbital position).
- Multi-Channel Synoptic Layers:
  * Thermal Infrared (TIR1): Cloud-top temperature and cyclone vortex tracking.
  * Precipitation Radar: Live Doppler reflectivity streams for active rain cells.
  * Wind Streamlines: Dynamic wind circulation and monsoon pressure vectors.
- Plain-Language Telemetry Analysis: Translates complex satellite scans into simple sentences
  explaining exactly what the satellite sees over the user's district.

[FEATURE 6: Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) Smart Crop Phenology & GDD Engine]
- Growing Degree Days (GDD) Calculation: Thermal accumulation formula based on daily max/min
  temperatures and base thresholds.
- Indian Crop Catalog: Paddy (Rice), Cotton, Wheat, Mustard, Sugarcane, Soybean.
- Days After Sowing (DAS) Slider: Tracks stages from Tillering, Panicle Initiation, Flowering,
  to Maturity.
- Pest & Disease Early Warning: Identifies weather triggers for Blast, False Smut, Pink
  Bollworm, and Yellow Rust with recommended bio-pesticide and chemical dosages.

[FEATURE 7: NDMA Multi-Hazard Disaster & Relief Camp Directory]
- Rainfall Inundation Simulator: Interactive slider (10mm to 200mm) modeling flood runoff,
  drainage overload, and waterlogging envelopes.
- 1-Tap SOS Speed Dialers: Direct calling links for 112 (National Emergency), 1077 (District
  Control), 1070 (SDMA), and 108 (Ambulance).
- Relief Camps Registry: Active designated municipal shelter locations with bed capacity and
  medical station flags.

[FEATURE 8: Rural 2G/3G SMS & Automated IVR Voice Broadcast]
- Direct Real-Number WhatsApp Dispatch: 1-click button opening WhatsApp with pre-formatted
  localized weather bulletins ready to send to any mobile number.
- Native Device SMS Trigger: Opens default SMS messenger app with standard 160-character
  cellular text alerts.
- Automated IVR Voice Simulation: SpeechSynthesis voice briefing in farmer's regional dialect.

[FEATURE 9: 1-Click Agro-Meteorological Bulletin Exporter]
- IMD-Branded Printable & PDF Bulletins: Formatted document with 3-day forecast, spraying
  advisory, and emergency warnings.
- Share to WhatsApp: Formatted text snippet ready for farmer WhatsApp groups.

[FEATURE 10: 30-Year Climate Benchmark & Monsoon Deviation]
- 1991–2020 Long-Period Average (LPA): Compares live district temperature against the 30-year
  normal baseline to detect micro-climate warming or monsoon delays.

[FEATURE 11: Persistent Geolocation & Live Background Auto-Sync Engine]
- Silent GPS & LocalStorage State: Checks browser coordinates and caches active location in
  localStorage. The platform never resets unexpectedly to "Delhi" on refresh.
- Live Background Polling: Synchronizes observations every 2.5 minutes in the background with
  a visual status indicator (`🟢 Auto-Sync • 12s ago [↻]`).
